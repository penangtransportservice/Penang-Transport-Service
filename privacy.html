Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Penang Transportation Service (SSM No. 201603049013 (PG0386063D)) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit this website or contact us, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia.

2. Personal data we collect

We only collect information that you provide yourself when you use the booking form or contact us, such as:

  • Full name
  • Travel date and number of passengers
  • Pickup location and destination
  • Phone/WhatsApp number and any other details you share in the conversation

We also automatically collect technical and usage data through Google Analytics, such as pages visited, length of visit, device and browser type, approximate location and IP address.

3. How the form on this website works

The form on this website does not send or store your data on our servers. When you press the "Send via WhatsApp" button, your details are placed in a message and opened in WhatsApp for you to send yourself. Once sent, the message is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

4. How we use your data

Your data is used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotations
  • Confirm and manage transport bookings
  • Provide customer service and support
  • Analyse website usage to improve its content and performance
  • Comply with legal requirements

5. Disclosure to others

We do not sell your personal data. Data is only shared with the drivers or partners needed to carry out your booking, or with authorities where required by law.

6. Third-party services, cookies and browser storage

This website uses third-party services, including Google Analytics (usage analytics), Google Fonts (font loading), Google Maps (location map), the Google Review link and WhatsApp. These parties may receive technical data such as your IP address and are subject to their own privacy policies.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to help us understand how visitors use this website. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

This website also uses temporary browser storage (sessionStorage) to remember which section to display; it contains no personal data and is cleared when the tab is closed.

7. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data against loss, misuse or unauthorised access. Data is kept only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent or limit the use of your data

To do so, please contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest update date is shown at the top of this page.
