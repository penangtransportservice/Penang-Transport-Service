Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains how cancellations, booking changes and refunds are handled by Penang Transportation Service. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

2. Cancellation by the customer

Refunds depend on when you cancel before the agreed pickup time:

  • More than 24 hours before pickup: full refund
  • 12 to 24 hours before pickup: 50% refund
  • Less than 12 hours before pickup or no-show: no refund

Cancellations must be notified via WhatsApp, phone or email.

3. Changing your booking

You may change the date, time or location at no charge if notified at least 24 hours before pickup, subject to vehicle availability. Later changes may incur a charge.

4. Cancellation by us

If we have to cancel your booking, for example due to a vehicle breakdown or circumstances beyond our control, we will offer a replacement vehicle, a new schedule or a full refund.

5. Flight delays or cancellations

If you provide your flight number at booking, we will adjust the pickup time at no charge when your flight is delayed. If your flight is cancelled and you provide proof, we will reschedule or refund you.

6. Service issues

If the service was not as agreed, please tell us within 48 hours after your trip. We will review your complaint and may offer a partial or full refund at our discretion.

7. Non-refundable items

  • Tolls, parking and third-party charges already paid on your behalf
  • Trips that have been completed or have started
  • Bookings missed without notice

8. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are returned via the original payment method within 7 to 14 working days.
